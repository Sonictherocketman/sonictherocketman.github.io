It feels good to be back.

Today my band, [The Fourth Section][ch], has released the first new single leading up to our upcoming second album. The single is called *From Chicago, With Love* and was inspired by the story of a woman I met during a visit to Chicago several years ago. I'm especially proud of the artwork. We made it ourselves.

If you like the song, please feel free to get in touch. We always love to hear from fans.

We've been working really hard since the release of our first album *Paper Peaks* and I can't wait to announce what we have coming. For now though, I hope everyone out there the best.

From San Diego,<br>
With Love
